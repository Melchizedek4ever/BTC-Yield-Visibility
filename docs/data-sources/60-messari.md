# Messari

**Tier:** 5 — Fundamentals/research (optional)
**Category:** Fundamentals
**Adapter kind:** n/a
**Status:** not recommended now

## What it is

Institutional-grade crypto research/data platform: asset fundamentals,
on-chain metrics for 200+ DeFi protocols, research reports, governance and
treasury data.

## Why it's interesting, why not now

Genuinely relevant to the charter's "Yield Sustainability" risk sub-factor —
treasury/governance data could inform whether a protocol's yield is
funded sustainably vs. from a depleting token treasury. But this is a paid,
enterprise-oriented API (`docs.messari.io`), and none of our 11 protocols
are likely to have deep Messari coverage at their current scale (most are
Stacks-ecosystem-specific, sub-$100M TVL protocols, not top-tier
Messari-profiled assets).

## Recommendation

Skip. Revisit only if the product moves toward Phase 3 (protocol
distribution) or Phase 4 (SDK) where broader multi-chain fundamentals
credibility matters more than it does for an 11-protocol Stacks-focused v1.
